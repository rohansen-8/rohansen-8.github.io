## About

I am currently a **Postdoctoral Researcher** with [FinStaR (Financial Statistics Research) at HKUST](https://finstar.hk/#home), under the mentorship of [Yingying Li](https://yyli.finstar.hk/) and [Xinghua Zheng](https://xhzheng.people.ust.hk/). My research focuses on **kernel methods, financial econometrics, and statistical learning**, with applications to asset pricing and nonparametric inference.

I received my PhD in Econometrics and Statistics from [Università della Svizzera italiana (USI)](https://www.usi.ch/en), supervised by [Paul Schneider](https://sites.google.com/view/paul-schneider) and co-supervised by [Michael Multerer](https://muchip.github.io). In 2025, I was a Visiting Student Researcher at [AFTLab, Stanford University](https://fintech.stanford.edu/), hosted by [Markus Pelger](https://mpelger.people.stanford.edu/).

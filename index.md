## About

I work on **kernel methods, financial econometrics, and statistical learning**, with applications to asset pricing and nonparametric inference.

I completed my PhD in Econometrics and Statistics at [Università della Svizzera italiana (USI)](https://www.usi.ch/en), supervised by [Paul Schneider](https://sites.google.com/view/paul-schneider) and co-supervised by [Michael Multerer](https://muchip.github.io). In 2025, I was a Visiting Student Researcher at [AFTLab, Stanford University](https://fintech.stanford.edu/), hosted by [Markus Pelger](https://mpelger.people.stanford.edu/).
